Accessibility statement for Replacing CMS tools

This is a temporary accessibility statement, last updated on 24 June 2026 and published on 26 June 2026.

A full statement, endorsed by the CPS Digital Accessibility Specialists, is in preparation and will replace this version by 30 September 2026.

What Replacing CMS tools do

The Replacing CMS tools are a modernisation of our case management tools within CPS. They currently focus on pre-charge activities, starting with task management through to a triage or review completion.

What you should be able to do

Replacing CMS tools are provided by the Crown Prosecution Service Digital Information Directorate, working alongside Case Strategy, Legal and Policy, and Continuous Improvement. We want everyone to be able to use them.

When using Replacing CMS tools, you should be able to:

  • change colours, contrast levels and fonts,
  • zoom in up to 400% without the text spilling off the screen,
  • navigate the screens using just a keyboard,
  • navigate the screens using speech recognition software (currently Dragon and Voice Access),
  • listen to and navigate the screens using a screen reader (currently JAWS, Narrator, and Read and Write),
  • interact with the displayed text, ensuring that it is as simple as possible to understand and assured that the layout follows a logical order.

Compliance status

The Crown Prosecution Service Digital Accessibility Specialist team has assessed this product against the WCAG 2.2 AA Standards, and as a result Replacing CMS tools has achieved a rating of bronze.

Despite our best efforts to ensure accessibility, there may be outstanding limitations, but we have minimised the volume and impact of these on the user. Below is a description of known limitations:

Within the next 3-6 months we are expecting to address:

  • JAWS - cannot read the loading spinner on any page that can display it
  • JAWS - there are known issues across check boxes and radio buttons not being contained within groups across the tools

Within the next 6 months we are expecting to address:

  • Dragon - Dragon cannot access date pickers across the tools
  • Keyboard or screen readers (JAWS, Windows Narrator) - the skip to content link can have issues on some screens
  • JAWS and keyboard navigation - action plan has issues not trapping the keyboard
  • some spillage at 400% zoom with table elements
  • at the present moment the only colour contrast is the Microsoft High Contrast modes

Longer term we will aim to address:

  • PDF documents will not colour contrast even with standard browser contrasting
